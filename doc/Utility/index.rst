.. _UTILITY:

OSVVM Utility Library
#####################

The OSVVM utility and verification component libraries were developed and are maintained by Jim Lewis of SynthWorks VHDL
Training. These libraries evolved from methodology and packages developed for SynthWorks' VHDL Testbenches and
verification class.

The packages are a work in progress and will be updated from time to time. Caution, undocumented items are experimental
and may be removed in a future version.

Please support OSVVM by purchasing your VHDL training from SynthWorks.
